Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to contribute original research articles that illustrate novel findings, significant projects, comprehensive surveys or industrial experiences in Smart Technologies and Intelligent Computing.

Submissions should highlight advancements, challenges and opportunities within the following domains:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Advanced Networked Systems and Communication Technologies
  • Machine Vision and Smart Perception
  • Emerging Trends in Computer Technologies

Interested authors are invited to submit full papers including results, figures and references. Papers will be accepted only by electronic submission through the Microsoft CMT link below.

Plagiarism Policy

All manuscripts submitted to IC2INS-2027 follow a double-blind review process, and authors are instructed not to include their details while submitting the manuscript.

IC2INS-2027 takes plagiarism very seriously and regards it as professional misconduct. Papers are screened for plagiarism, and any paper found to contain plagiarised content will be rejected. Only papers detected with less than 15% similarity (including references and DOI numbers) are carried forward to the next stage of review. No AI-generated content (such as from ChatGPT) is allowed — if AI-generated content is found at any stage, the paper will be rejected, even after successful registration.
