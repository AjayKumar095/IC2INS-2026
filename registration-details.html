Registration Details

Registration opens once your paper has been accepted through Microsoft CMT. Category-wise fees will be published here shortly.

Registration fee categories (Indian authors, foreign authors, industry delegates and students) are being finalised and will be announced on this page. For an update ahead of the public announcement, please reach out via the Contact page.

How registration works

1

Submit your paper

Upload your manuscript through the Microsoft CMT portal for double-blind review.

2

Receive the acceptance decision

Once your paper clears review and the plagiarism check, you'll receive an acceptance notification.

3

Complete registration

At least one author per accepted paper must register before the camera-ready deadline for the paper to be included in the proceedings.

4

Present at the conference

Join us on campus at Geeta University, Panipat on 10–11 September 2026.

Questions about registration?

Get in touch with the conference secretariat — we're happy to help with fee categories, invoicing and visa-support letters.
