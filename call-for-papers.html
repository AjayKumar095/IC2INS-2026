Call for Papers

IC2INS-2027 invites original, unpublished research at the intersection of computational intelligence and networked systems.

The 1st International Conference on Computational Intelligence and Networked Systems (IC2INS-2027) is a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals and scholars to present findings, discuss emerging trends, and explore future directions in intelligent computing and networked technologies. The conference welcomes contributions that integrate intelligent algorithms with robust network infrastructure to solve real-world problems in healthcare, smart cities, cybersecurity and autonomous systems.

Subject to approval by the IEEE Delhi Section, all accepted and presented papers will be submitted for possible inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.

Tracks & Topics of Interest

Artificial Intelligence Advanced Networked Systems & Communication Technologies Machine Vision & Smart Perception Emerging Trends in Computer Technologies

Within these tracks, authors are encouraged to submit work spanning neural networks and deep learning, IoT and 5G/6G networks, machine vision and cyber-physical systems, and AI-driven applications in medical and urban infrastructure.
