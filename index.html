About the conference

Three editions of research, industry and community

Since its founding in 1997, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Rajalakshmi Engineering College has built a steady record in teaching and research. Recognised by Anna University as a Collaborative Research Centre, the department runs M.S. (by research) and Ph.D. programmes alongside active industry partnerships for training, faculty development and joint R&D. ICCDS 2026 is the department's third edition, continuing its focus on original, unpublished research across computing and data science.
