About the conference

About IC2INS-2027

Welcome to IC2INS-2027: the 1st International Conference on Computational Intelligence and Networked Systems, organised by the School of Computer Science and Engineering, during 10th–11th September 2027 at Geeta University, Campus – Panipat, Haryana, India.

IC2INS-2027 is a premier international event aimed at fostering innovation, collaboration and knowledge exchange in the fields of intelligent computing, artificial intelligence and networked technologies. The conference serves as a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals and scholars to present their latest findings, discuss emerging trends and explore future directions in computational intelligence and intelligent communication systems. In a world increasingly driven by automation, digital transformation and smart systems, IC2INS-2027 addresses the pressing need to integrate intelligent algorithms with robust network infrastructure to solve complex, real-world problems.

The conference encourages a synergistic blend of theory and application, with an emphasis on impactful, interdisciplinary research that can drive advancements in areas such as healthcare, smart cities, cybersecurity and autonomous systems. IC2INS-2027 is committed to academic excellence and aims to promote international collaboration by connecting participants with global thought leaders, encouraging thought-provoking discussion, and contributing to the evolution of next-generation computing and network paradigms.

Subject to approval by the IEEE Delhi Section, all accepted and presented papers will be submitted for possible inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.

Conference Objectives

  • Explore the convergence of computational intelligence and next-generation networked systems, addressing the demands of automation, digital transformation and intelligent connectivity in modern society.
  • Promote interdisciplinary research that bridges artificial intelligence, communication technologies, machine vision and cyber-physical systems for solving complex, real-world problems.
  • Disseminate cutting-edge developments in neural networks, deep learning, IoT, 5G/6G networks, and AI-driven medical and urban infrastructure applications.
  • Foster collaboration between academia and industry, enabling technology transfer, startup engagement and co-development of smart, sustainable solutions.
  • Provide a platform for practical knowledge exchange — showcasing case studies, scalable AI models and intelligent communication architectures across healthcare, transportation and security.
  • Prepare young researchers for future challenges, encouraging engagement with global experts and deeper understanding of emerging computational paradigms.
