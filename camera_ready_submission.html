Camera Ready Submission

Instructions for the authors of accepted papers. Please review both the General and Required categories carefully before submitting your final manuscript to IEEE Xplore.

Before You Begin

General Instructions

  • Remove header and footer (if any) in your final camera ready manuscript.
  • Do not put any page numbers in the camera ready manuscript.
  • Remove titles such as Prof., Dr., Mr., Ms., etc. before the author name(s).
  • Remove hyperlinks (if any) in the final version of the manuscript.
  • Format the paper using the Proceeding standard template.
  • If using the Proceeding LaTeX template, click here.
  • For the Word template, click here.
Deadlines

Required Instructions

All three items below are mandatory for your paper to be included in the final proceedings.

June 25, 2027
  Camera Ready / Final Manuscript
June 25, 2027
  IEEE eCopyright Form (via email from IEEE)
August 8, 2027
  eRegistration Form, Duly Completed
Process 1

IEEE PDF eXpress Plus — Camera Ready Submission

  • Step 1: Revise your paper(s) according to the reviewers' comments, failing which it will be REJECTED.
  • Step 2: Download the IEEE Camera Ready Template for IC2INS-2027 — Word / LaTeX. For more templates, click here. The paper length should not exceed six (6) pages in IEEE two (2) column format. You must modify your paper exactly as per the format requirements specified in the Camera Ready Format.
  • Step 3 (Mandatory): Use the IEEE PDF eXpress Plus link below to check that your Camera Ready paper is IEEE compliant. Enter (Conference ID will be updated soon) as the Conference ID. IEEE PDF eXpress Plus for Camera Ready Paper Submission — IC2INS-2027.
  • Step 4: Once your Camera Ready PDF passes the check, download the PDF file certified by IEEE PDF eXpress Plus and upload the same on your Microsoft CMT account. Microsoft CMT will only accept the PDF file certified by IEEE PDF eXpress Plus — files in other formats cannot be uploaded to Microsoft CMT.
Process 2

IEEE Copyright Form Submission

  • Step 1: IC2INS-2027 supports submission of the eCopyright form through your Microsoft CMT account, failing which you will not be able to upload your final manuscript(s).
  • Step 2: Kindly answer all questions asked during submission of the eCopyright form.
Process 3

eRegistration Process

At least one author must register on or before August 8, 2027 to include the paper in the conference.

  • Step 1: Before starting eRegistration, please have copies of the following documents ready:
    • Copy of Student ID card
    • Copy of professional membership (IEEE)
    • Copy of bank transaction receipt / screenshot of online payment done
  • Step 2: To start the eRegistration process, click here (to be updated).
  • Step 3: Authors claiming a discount for IEEE membership, student status, or both, must submit a scanned/PDF copy of the proof to iccins@geetauniversity.edu.in on or before August 8, 2027.

Questions about your camera ready submission?

Reach out to the organising team if you need help with templates, IEEE PDF eXpress, or your CMT submission.
